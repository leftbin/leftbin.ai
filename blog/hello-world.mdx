# Building AI Agent Workflows That Actually Work

The promise of AI automation is everywhere. The delivery is rare.

At Leftbin, we build AI agent-powered workflows that run real business operations. Not demos. Not proofs of concept. Working software that handles the messy, judgment-heavy tasks your team does every day.

## Our Approach

**Deterministic where deterministic.** If a step has clear rules, encode them. Don't burn tokens on decisions a simple function can make.

**Agent where messy judgment.** When the task requires understanding context, weighing options, or synthesizing information — that's where agents earn their keep.

**Human where risk.** High-stakes decisions get human approval. The agent prepares everything; the human makes the call.

This isn't a philosophy slide. It's how we architect every workflow we build.
